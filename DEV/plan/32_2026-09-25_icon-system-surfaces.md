# The product's system surfaces follow the platform's shape rules

**Status:** Draft
**Priority:** 45
**Date:** 2026-09-25

> Carried forward from [ticket 24](done/24_2026-09-23_contract-iconography-sync.md), which adopted the
> shared icon vocabulary for every in-page glyph and left the surfaces Windows and the browser draw for
> the product. `ICON-RENDER` rule 9 (0.12 for Windows, 0.13 for the extension action icon) names them, and
> the catalog registry holds this product's dated exception for them until 2026-12-31. Contracts: `ICON-RENDER`
> 0.13, `ICON-SET` 0.15 (pointers in [`docs/contracts/`](../../docs/contracts/)).

## What / why

Everything a page draws is now a vocabulary glyph. What the operating system and the browser draw for the
product is not: the program icon, the Store tiles, the icon of a registered document type, the Explorer
"Convert to HTML" entry and the extension's toolbar button all show the "DOC HTML" / "DH" lettering on
navy, or reuse the exe icon. At 16 px the lettering does not read, Windows gets no unplated or light-theme
tile, and the Explorer entry and the toolbar button sit on a light or dark background the product cannot
see, so rule 2 (colour from the theme) cannot hold there - rule 9 says what holds instead.

## Findings (read 2026-09-25)

- **MSIX tiles:** `msix/build-msix.ps1` renders `StoreLogo`, `Square44x44Logo` ("DH", 44 px),
  `Square71x71Logo`, `Square150x150Logo` and the wide tile from text on the brand colour. There is no
  `targetsize-16/24/32/48/256` form, no `altform-unplated` / `altform-lightunplated`, and the package has no
  `resources.pri` that would let Windows resolve them.
- **ICO:** the executable and installer icon is the lettering; rule 9 wants 16 to 256 px, with the 16 px
  form legible.
- **Explorer:** the "Convert to HTML" verb and the registered document types (`internal/windowsreg`) show
  the exe icon. Rule 9: the verb shows its meaning's glyph, `action.convert` (0.15, Material `transform`),
  in the mono look in one tone `#808080` at 16 px with 20, 24 and 32 px forms; a document type shows its
  meaning's glyph the same way, or the product mark where the vocabulary has none.
- **Extension action icon:** `extension/manifest.json` names 16, 32, 48 and 128 px icons of the same
  lettering. Rule 9 (0.13): the mark on its own plate, or the one-tone mono look, holding 3 : 1 on a light
  and a dark toolbar.

## Direction

1. Decide the mark first (ticket 24's open question 5): a glyph-based mark legible at 16 px with a
   monochrome form, or the lettering kept for the large sizes with a simplified small form. The mark is
   product artwork (`ICON-SET` rule 7); only its platform forms are the contract's.
2. MSIX: the `targetsize-*` and `altform-*` set, generated by the build, and a `resources.pri` built with
   `makepri`; checked by installing the package and reading the taskbar and Start at 100 % and 200 %.
3. ICO: 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 256 px frames.
4. Explorer: the verb icon from `assets/glyphs/action.convert.svg` (vendored when this ticket starts) in
   `#808080`, rendered to an ICO; the document-type icon likewise or the mark.
5. Extension: the action icon set, checked on a light and a dark Chrome and Edge toolbar.
6. Close the `ICON-RENDER` rule 9 row of this product in the catalog registry, and move the "System
   surfaces" rows of [`docs/GLYPH-MAP.md`](../../docs/GLYPH-MAP.md) to `conforms` / `artwork`.

## Done criteria

- [ ] The mark is decided and recorded here.
- [ ] The MSIX carries the `targetsize-*` / `altform-*` tiles resolved through `resources.pri`, seen on the

- [ ] The ICO has every rule 9 size; the 16 px frame is legible.
- [ ] The Explorer verb shows `action.convert` in `#808080`, measured at 3 : 1 on the light and the dark menu.
- [ ] The extension action icon holds 3 : 1 on a light and a dark toolbar.
- [ ] The registry exception is closed and the glyph map updated.
